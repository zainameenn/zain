### H2: How to use the TikTok bio generator

A TikTok bio is tiny, so every word has to earn its spot. The tool does the cutting for you.

1. **Pick your account type.** Creator, business or personal. A recipe account and a boxing gym need very different twelve words.
2. **Say what you do and who it's for.** Pick one audience. "Students cooking on a budget" beats "foodies, students, parents and everyone".
3. **Add what you want to be known for.** A topic, a city if you're local, and one real detail like how often you post.
4. **Preview, copy, paste.** Click Preview to see each bio on a TikTok style profile. Copy your favorite, then in TikTok tap Edit profile and paste it into Bio.

### H2: What makes a good TikTok bio

Viewers usually land on your profile after one video they liked. The bio answers the only question they have: will the next video be this good too?

You get about a dozen words, roughly the dialogue in a whole Mr. Bean episode, so a good TikTok bio does just three things:

1. **Names your thing.** Budget recipes, resume fixes, beginner boxing. One thing.
2. **Says who it's for,** if there's room. "For students" or "for total beginners" helps the right people self select.
3. **Gives a tiny reason to follow,** like "new recipe daily" or "part 2 is up".

**ONE THING, SAID CLEARLY.** If your bio tries to cover three niches, nobody remembers any of them.

### H2: Your TikTok profile: what goes where

The bio is only one part of the profile. Knowing what the other parts do saves you from cramming everything into the bio.

| Profile part | What it does | Quick tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Username | Your @handle, used for tags and search | 2 to 24 characters: letters, numbers, underscores and periods |
| Name | Shows above your bio | Your name or brand, plus what you do if it fits |
| Bio | Tells viewers why to follow | One thing, said clearly |
| Website link | Sends people to your site or shop | Needs 1,000 followers or a Verified Business Account |
| Pinned videos | Your best first impression | Pin the video that explains your account best |

TikTok's help center says you can [add a website link](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581821550328896012) once you have at least 1,000 followers or a Verified Business Account, and that links which open other social media apps are no longer supported. Its [username rules](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581821550156962315) allow 2 to 24 characters, and you can change your username once every 30 days.

### H2: TikTok bio length and formatting

TikTok bios have always been short, and this tool writes every bio in **80 characters** or fewer, counting spaces and emoji. Some newer versions of the app may allow more, but 80 fits everywhere, and short bios read better under a video anyway.

A few habits that help:

1. **Keep it to one line.** In a space this small, a line break costs you words.
2. **Don't type a URL into the bio.** Use the website link on your profile once you qualify, and point to it with "link below".
3. **Use one emoji at most,** ideally one that replaces a word, like 🥊 for boxing.
4. **Skip your name.** It already shows above the bio.

### H2: TikTok bio mistakes to avoid

1. **Trying to say everything.** Three niches in 80 characters means none of them land.
2. **A link nobody can tap.** URLs typed into the bio just sit there as text.
3. **Copying a trending bio.** If ten thousand accounts say "living my best life ✨", yours disappears.
4. **A bio that doesn't match your videos.** If the bio says fitness and the feed is all cooking, viewers leave confused.
5. **Never updating it.** New series, new product, new city? Change the bio the same day.
