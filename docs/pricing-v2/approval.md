## Terms I wrote that need your OK
1. Revisions: 1 round per social content piece on Essentials and Growth, 2 on Plus.
2. Billing: monthly work billed weekly or every two weeks (kept from the old site), one time services billed before they start.
3. Cancellation: "no long contracts, notice terms agreed in writing before we start". No notice period is stated.
4. Ads: no setup fee by default; large rebuilds quoted first.
5. Implementation: "I make the changes when I have access" for SEO.
6. Ownership: clients own ad accounts and content made for them.
7. Reddit post and comment numbers are ceilings, not quotas.
8. Complete Growth covers up to two active channels at a time.
9. Labels used instead of "Most popular": "For growing businesses", "For steady presence", "For Google and Meta", "For a full plan", "For several channels".
