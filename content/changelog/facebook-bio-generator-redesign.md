## What's new

**No typing needed.** Choose Personal Profile, Business Page, Creator or Facebook Group, then answer four quick questions from dropdowns. Each question has an Other option if nothing fits.

**An optional note.** Add a sentence about yourself, your business or your group, and the bios use it as the main source of details.

**Tone, language and emoji.** Pick from six tones, write in English, Hindi, Urdu or Bangla, and add an emoji only if you want one.

**Three editable bios.** Each comes with a character counter, a copy button and a live Intro preview. Group descriptions get more room, since that field is longer than a profile Intro.

**A new page.** Writing tips, examples you can copy, the difference between a bio and the About section, and answers to common questions.
