## Less scrolling through endless links

The footer has been reorganized to make the most useful destinations easier to find.

Instead of listing every free tool individually, visitors can now open the complete tools directory.

## What's improved

- Clearer navigation groups.
- Direct links to services.
- Easier access to case studies.
- Blog and free tools links.
- A new changelog link.
- A sitemap link.
- Simplified mobile navigation.
- Improved spacing and readability.
