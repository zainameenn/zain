## Making pages easier to discover

The website now has a human readable [sitemap](/sitemap) organized by section.

Behind the scenes, the [XML sitemap](/sitemap.xml) is built from the site's published pages.

When a new public page is added, it appears in the sitemap automatically.

When an existing page receives a meaningful update, the sitemap uses its actual modification date.

This makes the site's structure clearer for visitors and gives search engines an updated list of preferred URLs.
