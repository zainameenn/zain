## Clearer prices and clearer services

The pricing page put the monthly plans and one time options in one place, so visitors didn't have to search through individual service pages to understand the costs.

It also links to the relevant service pages for more detailed information.

The page was redesigned on October 24, 2026 with packages for every service. See [A Better Way to Compare Marketing Packages](/changelog/pricing-page-redesign).
