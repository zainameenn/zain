## What's new

Twenty more free tools, bringing the total to 83.

- [Canonical URL Checker](/tools/canonical-url-checker) and [Redirect Chain Checker](/tools/redirect-chain-checker): check a public URL's canonical and every redirect hop.

## How they work

Thirteen of these run entirely in your browser with no AI. The canonical and redirect checkers fetch only the public URL you enter, and refuse private or internal addresses. The writing tools never invent prices, discounts, reviews, metrics or search volumes: answers that do are rejected before you see them.
