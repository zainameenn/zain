## A place to see what's changing

I've added a changelog so important website updates don't disappear after they're published.

Each update now has its own page, with details, screenshots and links to the relevant feature or resource.

You can browse updates chronologically, filter them by category or search them. There's also an [RSS feed](/changelog/rss.xml).

The changelog also provides a record of improvements made across the website.
