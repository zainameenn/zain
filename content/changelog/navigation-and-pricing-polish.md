## What changed

A handful of smaller improvements went live on October 25, 2026.

- **Free Tools menu.** Hovering Free Tools in the menu now opens a panel with every tool, grouped by what it's for. On phones it opens as a list inside the menu.
- **Pricing cards.** The one time service cards on the [pricing page](/pricing) now share the same header height, so titles, prices and buttons line up. The Complete Growth section has larger, easier to read text.
- **Mobile fix.** Pricing comparison tables no longer make the page scroll sideways on phones.
- **Footer.** The link columns are evenly spaced, the description sits on three lines, and the social icons are arranged in two rows of five, now including Substack.
- **Changelog.** Screenshots in the list show in full, and more updates load automatically as you scroll.
- **Instagram tools.** The live phone preview is back on the Instagram Bio and Caption generators.
