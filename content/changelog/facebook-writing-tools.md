## Better introductions and clearer posts

The [Facebook Bio Generator](/tools/facebook-bio-generator) helps explain who you are or what your business offers. It has been live since October 13, 2026.

The [Facebook Caption Generator](/tools/facebook-caption-generator) joined it on October 18, 2026. It helps turn updates, announcements, product information and other post ideas into readable content.

Both tools use your own information to make the results more relevant.
