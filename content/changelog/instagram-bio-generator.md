## A bio that explains who you are

An Instagram bio has limited space, so every word needs a reason to be there.

The generator helps creators, personal accounts and businesses describe what they do in a way that feels natural.

## What you can customize

- Account type.
- What you do or sell.
- Target audience.
- Keywords and location.
- Relevant achievements.
- Tone and formatting.
- Next action for profile visitors.

The tool also shows an Instagram style preview so you can see how the text fits before copying it.
