## Captions should match the post

A Reel, carousel and product photo do not need identical captions.

The generator uses the information you provide to write a caption that fits the format and purpose of your content.

## What you can control

- Content topic.
- Post format.
- Target audience.
- Tone.
- Intended action.
- Emoji and hashtag preferences.

The focus is on writing something relevant to the content rather than filling space with generic phrases.
