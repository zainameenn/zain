## What's new

Ten more free tools, bringing the total to 43.

**Calculators and checkers (no AI, run in your browser)**

- [Etsy Fee Calculator](/tools/etsy-fee-calculator): listing, transaction, processing and Offsite Ads fees with your profit and margin, using Etsy's published rates for the US, UK, Germany, Canada and Australia.
- [Customer Acquisition Cost Calculator](/tools/customer-acquisition-cost-calculator): blended CAC with the formula shown, an optional cost breakdown and an LTV to CAC ratio when you know your LTV.
- [Robots.txt Tester](/tools/robots-txt-tester): checks whether a crawler may request a URL and shows the exact rule that decides it, following RFC 9309.

**Writing and planning tools**

- [Newsletter Name Generator](/tools/newsletter-name-generator) and [Podcast Name Generator](/tools/podcast-name-generator): one name with a short tagline.
- [Domain Name Ideas Generator](/tools/domain-name-ideas-generator): one domain idea in the extension you pick.
- [Shopify Product Title Generator](/tools/shopify-product-title-generator): one clear product title from your real details, with an optional SEO title.
- [YouTube Video Script Generator](/tools/youtube-video-script-generator): one full script with sections, a word count and an estimated length.
- [Local Business FAQ Generator](/tools/local-business-faq-generator): five to eight editable FAQs, with answers that need a missing fact flagged for you.
- [SEO Keyword Cluster Generator](/tools/seo-keyword-cluster-generator): group keywords by shared wording instantly in your browser, or by topic and intent with AI, then export a CSV.

## How they work

Every writing tool gives one result you can edit, copy or regenerate. None of them claims a name is available, invents prices or policies, or makes up search volume.
