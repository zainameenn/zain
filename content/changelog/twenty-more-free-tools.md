## What's new

Twenty more free tools, bringing the total to 63.

**Names and handles**

- [Instagram Username Generator](/tools/instagram-username-generator) and [TikTok Username Generator](/tools/tiktok-username-generator): one handle, checked against each platform's character rules.
- [Business Name Generator](/tools/business-name-generator), [Business Slogan Generator](/tools/business-slogan-generator) and [Blog Name Generator](/tools/blog-name-generator).

**YouTube**

- [YouTube Chapter Generator](/tools/youtube-chapters-generator): turns timestamps or SRT and WebVTT subtitles into chapters and checks YouTube's rules. Times are never guessed.
- [YouTube Thumbnail Text Generator](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-text-generator): one short overlay line that adds to your title.

**SEO and technical**

- [SEO Content Brief Generator](/tools/seo-content-brief-generator), with a Markdown download.
- [Robots.txt Generator](/tools/robots-txt-generator), [XML Sitemap Validator](/tools/xml-sitemap-validator) and [Local Business Schema Generator](/tools/local-business-schema-generator). These three run in your browser with no AI.

**Etsy and Shopify**

- [Shopify Collection Description Generator](/tools/shopify-collection-description-generator), [Etsy Shop Announcement Generator](/tools/etsy-shop-announcement-generator) and [Etsy Shop About Section Generator](/tools/etsy-shop-about-section-generator).

**Calculators**

- [Break-even ROAS Calculator](/tools/break-even-roas-calculator) and [LTV to CAC Ratio Calculator](/tools/ltv-cac-ratio-calculator), with the formulas shown.

**Copywriting**

- [Website Hero Headline Generator](/tools/website-hero-headline-generator), [SaaS Value Proposition Generator](/tools/saas-value-proposition-generator), [Email Preheader Generator](/tools/email-preheader-generator) and [Changelog Entry Generator](/tools/changelog-entry-generator).

## How they work

The writing tools give one result you can edit and copy. They don't claim names are available, invent prices or results, or pretend to have checked live search results. The calculators, validators and generators that don't need AI run entirely in your browser.
