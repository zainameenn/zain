## Less guessing about what's included

I redesigned the pricing experience to make packages easier to understand and compare.

## What's improved

- Clearer package names.
- Better organized pricing cards.
- More readable descriptions.
- Service specific deliverables.
- Comparison tables.
- Clearer distinctions between one time work and monthly services.
- Improved spacing and alignment.
- Better mobile layouts.

Every service now has three packages, and Complete Growth covers several channels in one plan.

## Why it matters

The right package should be easier to identify without scheduling a call just to understand what's included.
