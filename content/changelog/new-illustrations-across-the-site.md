## What's new

A new set of original illustrations, drawn in one consistent style.

**Free tools**

- Each of the 83 tools has its own drawing in its "how it works" section, showing what that tool actually does: a robots.txt rule being matched, an MRR breakdown, a SERP snippet, and so on.
- Non bio tools no longer show the small bio form previews (account type, style, "Copy bio") in their steps. Those now appear only on the bio generators.
- The tools directory explains the three steps with one drawing.

**The rest of the site**

- New drawings on the homepage hero, the "what a growth marketing specialist does", comparison, marketing leaks, one person and four step method sections.
- New service drawings on the services page and the SEO, Reddit and social media service pages.
- New about, pricing and contact drawings, plus phase diagrams on the Blainy and LoomPad case studies.

Results, screenshots, prices and reviews are unchanged. The drawings explain ideas; they don't stand in for proof.
