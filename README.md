## 🧰 Growth marketing services

If you already know the channel, great. If you don't, that's kind of my thing. Every service starts the same way: find what's actually blocking growth before spending time or money on a fix.

### 🧭 [SaaS growth consultant](https://www.zainameen.com/services/saas-growth-consultant)

Most stalled SaaS products don't need more channels. They need the one bottleneck found and fixed. As a SaaS growth consultant, I look at the whole system before touching any single part of it:

- **Positioning:** who it's for, what it replaces and why it's better. If this is fuzzy, every channel underperforms.
- **Offer:** what people get, what it costs and why now.
- **Acquisition:** the one or two channels that bring the right people, not seven half run ones.
- **Conversion:** what happens after someone lands. The page, the pitch and the next step.
- **Activation and retention:** getting signups to actually use the product, then keeping them.
- **Measurement and prioritization:** knowing which numbers matter and deciding what to fix first.

The process: diagnose, prioritize, position, build, test, then scale only what proves itself.

### 🔎 [SEO specialist for SaaS](https://www.zainameen.com/services/seo-specialist-for-saas)

SEO for SaaS that ranks on Google, Bing and in AI answers, and turns that traffic into signups. I work from search demand all the way to conversion:

- **Search demand:** what your buyers are actually looking for.
- **Intent:** separating research searches from buying searches, so each gets the right page.
- **Pages and content:** original content and landing pages built for that search.
- **Visibility:** technical SEO, internal linking and structure so Google, Bing and AI search can find and cite you.
- **Conversion:** qualified traffic turned into a trial, a demo or a signup.

SEO rarely works alone, so it's often paired with Reddit marketing and a clear growth strategy.

### 💬 [Reddit marketing specialist](https://www.zainameen.com/services/reddit-marketing-specialist)

Reddit marketing for SaaS with no ad spend and no bans. Your buyers already ask Reddit for recommendations, and Google and AI answers increasingly surface those threads. I help you show up there with something worth reading:

- **Research:** the right subreddits, their rules, recurring questions and competitor mentions.
- **Strategy:** which communities to prioritize, what to post, and when a product mention actually fits.
- **Execution:** posts, helpful comments and ongoing replies from real accounts.
- **Monitoring:** recommendation threads, community discussions and mentions of your brand.
- **Measurement:** views, traffic, users and conversions, reported monthly.

### 📱 [Social media marketing specialist](https://www.zainameen.com/services/social-media-marketing-specialist)

Social media marketing that brings visits and leads, not just likes. One person handles the five parts most accounts skip:

- **Strategy:** who you're talking to, which platforms matter and what each post is supposed to do.
- **Content:** posts that teach, show, prove or start a conversation.
- **Creative:** graphics and carousels designed by the same person who writes the posts.
- **Distribution and community:** showing up in the groups and communities where buyers ask for recommendations.
- **Measurement and iteration:** what got seen, clicked and brought leads, then more of what worked.

### 🎯 [Google and Meta ads specialist](https://www.zainameen.com/services/google-and-meta-ads-specialist)

Google Ads and Meta Ads management that fixes the funnel before spending more. Ads only scale when the offer, tracking and landing page are ready, so I work through all of it:

- **Offer and goal:** what we're asking someone to do, and why they'd want to.
- **Tracking:** real conversions recorded before launch, so every decision is based on data.
- **Audience and intent:** who should see the ad and what they're looking for.
- **Campaign structure:** campaigns, ad sets, keywords, negatives and budgets organized so the data means something.
- **Creative and landing page:** the ad earns the click, the page earns the conversion. I write and design both.
- **Optimization and scaling:** waste cut and budget moved every week into what proves itself.

You own the ad account and pay Google and Meta directly. My fee is separate from ad spend.
