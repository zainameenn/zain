## 11. Black Friday marketing checklist, NEW

**Eyebrow:** USEFUL EVEN IF YOU DON'T BOOK

## A practical Black Friday marketing checklist for 2026.

A seasonal sale is easier to promote when the offer is already clear. Before you spend on ads or publish more posts, make sure the page visitors land on is worth visiting.

### 1. Make one clear offer page

State what the customer gets, who it's for, the discount terms, and exactly what happens next. Avoid five competing CTAs above the fold.

### 2. Match the search intent

Use a clear page title, readable URL, H1 and opening paragraph that all describe the same specific offer. Not identical keyword stuffing, just consistent, useful wording.

For this page, the core idea is **Black Friday marketing deals**, so the main title, page URL, H1 and first sentence should all make that subject obvious.

### 3. Make service comparisons easy

People should be able to compare actual deliverables, prices, and terms without opening five tabs. Show the difference between one-time and recurring work.

### 4. Prepare content before the promotion

Plan the search page, email, social posts, and any relevant community participation. Adapt the message to each channel. Posting the exact same graphic everywhere isn't a strategy.

### 5. Test the path from click to inquiry

Check the page on mobile. Test the links, form, price calculations, and confirmation messages. Make sure you can tell which campaign brought an inquiry.

### 6. Plan what happens after the sale

Confirm how new projects start, what information the customer needs to provide, and which work begins first. Take down or clearly mark expired offers when the promotion ends.
